# What I Would Build Next for PURE Energy Dealers

> Aim for approximately 150 words.

If I joined PURE Energy as an MIS Assistant, the next thing I would build is a **Dealer Performance Dashboard with Monthly Sales Targets**.

**The Problem**

Currently, leads are assigned to dealers but there is no structured way to track whether dealers are actually following up on those leads, closing sales, or falling behind their monthly targets. Regional managers have no real-time visibility into which dealers need support.

**The Solution**

A lightweight reporting module that aggregates lead-to-conversion data per dealer, compares it against configurable monthly targets, and sends automated weekly email summaries to regional managers. Dealers would see their own numbers via a simplified read-only login.

**Dealer Benefit**

Dealers would know exactly where they stand against their targets each week, helping them prioritise follow-ups and reduce the time leads spend in the "Contacted" stage without progressing.

**How Success Would Be Measured**

- Reduction in average lead-to-conversion time (currently tracked manually)
- Increase in conversion rate across the dealer network
- Manager time saved on compiling monthly reports

This is a practical, high-value addition that builds directly on the existing lead data without requiring infrastructure changes.
